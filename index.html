About

I am a PhD candidate in Econometrics at the Asia-Europe Institute, Universiti Malaya, where I also work as a researcher on the institute's econometrics research. My thesis, Heterogeneous dynamic panel data model with extensions: Theory and applications, develops new heterogeneous dynamic panel data estimators — extending ARDL, Pooled Mean Group (PMG-ARDL), cross-sectionally augmented ARDL (CS-ARDL), and GMM methods, with theoretical extensions incorporating Fourier functions, asymmetric dynamics, and multifactor error structures.

Alongside this theoretical work, I write applied econometrics papers on energy, renewable energy, and critical minerals — using panel methods to study geopolitical risk, policy uncertainty, and the energy transition in ASEAN and East Asia. I'm open to collaborations on empirical modelling and code development, as well as manuscript writing and results documentation.
