Nexus of geo-political risk, policy uncertainties and renewable energy production: The moderating roles of governance components
Akhter, T.* & Arif, M.A. · Energy Reports
Published Scopus Q1 WoS Q1

Abstract

This paper examines how geopolitical risk and policy uncertainty affect renewable energy production across ASEAN and East Asian economies over 1990–2024, applying FMOLS and NARDL estimation, and tests whether governance quality moderates these effects.

Highlights

  • Covers ASEAN and East Asian economies, 1990–2024.
  • Estimated with FMOLS and NARDL.
  • Governance components moderate the impact of geopolitical risk and policy uncertainty on renewable energy production.

Genuine or spurious asymmetry? Disentangling uncertainty effects on renewable energy consumption using Fourier-augmented panel NARDL
Arif, M.A.* & Furuoka, F. · Energy Nexus
Published Scopus Q1 WoS Q1

Abstract

This paper tests whether asymmetric responses of renewable energy consumption to uncertainty are genuine or an artifact of unmodelled structural change, using a Fourier-augmented panel NARDL across 37 OECD countries over 1990–2024.

Highlights

  • Sample: 37 OECD countries, 1990–2024.
  • Uses a Fourier-augmented panel NARDL to separate genuine asymmetry from unmodelled structural breaks.
  • Geopolitical risk shows the strongest long-run asymmetric effect on renewable energy consumption.
